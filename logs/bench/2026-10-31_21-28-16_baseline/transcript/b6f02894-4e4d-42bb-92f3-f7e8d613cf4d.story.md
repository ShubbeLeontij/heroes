## Plan for the game

PLAN (Люди, start Чарил; opponents are random bots, so the race is my own VP)
- R1 (mine): wood, iron, move, build. Move to Улин (1w1g, foundation) with 1 MP spare: skip monster 2, kill monster 3 with both soldiers, take 2 iron. Build Ратуша on the Улин foundation. Expect 0w 3i 0g.
- Build order, adapted to each round's build level: Ратуша → Башня (L2) → Казармы / Таверна (L3) → Собор (L4); Рынок (L2) → Дом следопытов (L3) → Арсенал (L4); Гильдия магов (L4). Skip Гильдия воинов. Iron is the bottleneck (~32 needed); gold pays for the army.
- Rounds 5, 9, 13 (mine): gather, gather, move 3, build 4 → put an L4 building on a fresh foundation (+4 VP). End moves on foundations (Улин, Яскер, Эльри; Пиклин and Хагмат via Оррид; Есмор via Вернесс) so a build that comes before the move still lands on one.
- Army: knights after Казармы, used for monsters 2, 5, 8 (cheaper after Собор). Engine = Арсенал + Собор (+ Маги) by about R9–10: archmage 7 strength for 3g, knight 4 for 2g. Then each move round: monster 13/19/25/32 plus a fortress (Флисс needs 7, Ролгой 12, Юноро 18, Щерс 20). Loot + Рынок + Таверна nearly pay for these.
- Artifacts: Таскар (12w, next to Улин) if wood piles up — turns wood into gold. Шог Ид if 4 mountains come naturally. Содмур if I hold 3+ fortresses. Цурт only with spare iron. Use the Башня reservation (4w) if a bot threatens a target.
- Stockpile: cross the 14 mark (2 VP) before big spends; in R15–16 put leftovers into marks.
- PvP: a late win circles my leftmost monster (big VP), so take fights I'm sure to win; otherwise don't share a region while holding loot or an artifact (run threats.py).
- Гномы: strong units (Механик doubling); random attacks → keep a few units when a bot can reach my region.
- Нежить: cursed regions kill my weakest unit → check '(cursed)' before moving.
- Наги: krak strength is n², dangerous late; a lone Гидра forces a battle → commit 0. They can take Таскар, Цурт or Дорталь for 3 kraks → reserve the one I want.

## Round 1 · action_order

Round 1 order: **wood, iron, move, build**.

1. **Wood (level 1), then iron (level 2):** stock goes from 0w 1i 1g to 1w 3i 1g.
2. **Move (level 3):** I go to Улин (forest, costs 2 MP), which gives 1w 1g on the first visit and has a foundation. The spare 1 MP lets me skip monster 2. Both soldiers (strength 4) then beat monster 3 for 1 VP and 2 resources of my choice, and I'll take 2 iron.
3. **Build (level 4):** Ратуша (2w 2i 2g) on the Улин foundation for 1 VP. From then on every new region I visit gives +1 resource.

I tested this in the simulation against random bots. It ends at 2 VP with 0w 3i 0g, which is exactly the cost of Башня иллюзий. That means I can build it in round 2 if that round has a build at level 2 or higher.

**Alternatives I rejected:**
- **Move at level 1 to Яскер:** same leftover resources, but no spare MP. I'd have to use the soldiers on monster 2, which gives no loot, and I'd end in a dead-end region instead of the Улин hub.
- **Iron before wood, or gold instead of wood:** the same 3 resources are left over, but with less iron, so Башня in round 2 would depend on an iron gather coming before the build.
- **Рынок first:** it moves Ратуша's bonus later. Round 2 would also need an iron gather before the build, which happens only about 40% of the time.

**Risk:** with 3 MP, the Гномы and Наги bots could also reach Улин. If one comes and declares a fight, the player battle happens before the monster fight, so I'd still have my 2 soldiers to defend with. I hold almost nothing for them to take.

**Decision:** `["wood", "iron", "move", "build"]`

## Round 1 · build_recruit

Building **Ратуша** on the Улин foundation (1 VP).

**Why Ратуша, not Рынок:**
- **Faster economy:** every new region I visit from round 2 on gives +1 resource of my choice.
- **Unlocks the main chain:** Ратуша leads to Башня иллюзий, then Таверна and Казармы (level 3), then Собор (level 4). Казармы is my only way to hire units, short of the 0-VP Гильдия воинов. Собор makes units cheap.

The 1 VP difference also disappears if I later put a higher-level building on Улин. Гильдия воинов isn't useful: I have only 2 gold, enough for one soldier.
