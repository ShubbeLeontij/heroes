# Strategy notes: Люди

## General strategy
- Opening: Ратуша R1-2 (on a foundation if possible), Гильдия воинов, then Казармы (3 VP, via guild, no Башня needed) → Гильдия магов (L4, 4 VP) ASAP. Then Башня → Таверна → Собор; Рынок → Дом → Арсенал. All 10 buildings by ~R13 is achievable.
- Build every L3/L4 building while standing on a FRESH foundation (+level VP). Plan move→build rounds so you end on an unused foundation. Count: 5 foundations at 4 each = 20 VP.
- Таверна early: each monster circle is then worth +1. Fight monsters every move round with the minimal winning army; skip with unspent MP to the best value monster (8 for 9 str early is great).
- After Собор: archmage 3g = 5 str (7 with Арсенал) is the best strength per gold.
- Unit-less opponent in your region = free PvP win (1 unit) → monster circle + resources.
- Late game (R13+) keep a target list: fortresses (Юноро 17, Щерс 19) and monster 19/25; get strength there BEFORE rivals (Hydra grabs fortresses near lakes).
- Aim for one stockpile threshold (14 of one resource); ties are decided by resources in stock, so don't overspend in the last round.
