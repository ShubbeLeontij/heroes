# Strategy notes: Наги

## General strategy
- Наги score through buildings (each level 1, 1–3 VP), Гидра fortress captures, Храм summons, Плотина lakes and the three 3-Krak artifacts (Таскар, Цурт, Дорталь: 4 VP each). Foundations give only 1 VP each, so do not race opponents for them; place buildings on them only for Дворец (+1 per foundation building).
- Economy is the bottleneck: a Krak costs 4 gold or 5 wood/iron, and remainders sit idle. Prefer gold. As first player, put the gathers at levels 2–3 and the build after them. Visit unvisited regions with gold. Plan each build round so you reach exactly 3–4 Kraks.
- Artifacts: buy them early, before opponents gather 12 resources of one kind (the orcs took Таскар with 12 wood in round 11). The purchase happens in the move phase, so you need 3 Kraks already in stock when you end a move in the artifact's region. A round-1 artifact beats a building.
- Гидра: build Храм early (+2 VP per summon). Close the note pair as soon as you can. A lone Гидра takes fortresses for VP only (the loot is lost): 18 beats 6/7/10/11/14/17. Build Сердце океана by mid-game: at 25 she can take 19/21 fortresses (5 VP each). Without Маяк she moves 1 region per move round, so plan her route 2 rounds ahead. Summon her into a lake next to her target; Гейзер lakes give good spots.
- Гейзер (2 Kraks) + Плотина is strong: turn every region you end in into a lake if the spare MP costs nothing (I reached 7 lakes = 7 VP).
- Spending Kraks on strength-3 monsters is a bad trade: a Krak is worth more as currency.
- Start near the centre/gold (Вернесс portal: Таскар, Лорвельд, Есмор); avoid dead ends like Хагмат.

## Reminders
- [round 1, build_recruit, destination] If 3 Kraks and an artifact region (Цурт/Таскар/Дорталь) is reachable this move, keep the Kraks and buy the artifact (4 VP).
- [build_recruit] Get Храм early, then Сердце океана before mid-game, so Гидра can take the 19/21 fortresses.
- [make_lake] Say yes unless you need the MP: each lake is +1 VP with Плотина and a new Гидра summon spot.
- [destination] Do not end in a region with an armed opponent while you hold 0 units; they win for free.
- [action_order] Gathers (gold best) first, build after them; include a move when Гидра has a target.
