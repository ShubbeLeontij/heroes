# Strategy notes: Нежить

## General strategy
- Every terrain costs 2 MP: mountains are cheap for me. Early route Карракс→Лорвельд→Нокс→Шог Ид takes the Шог Ид artifact (+8 res) by round 4.
- Foundation VP is the biggest lever. Leaders score 16-17 from foundations, so put EVERY level 2+ building on a fresh foundation (marks are per player, so opponent-occupied ones still count). Plan the move so I end on an unbuilt foundation before each build. Foundations: Лорвельд, Нокс, Яскер, Эльри, Улин, Пиклин, Хагмат, Есмор, Асандер.
- Build order that worked: Кладбище → Алтарь → Курган → Часовня → Капище (5w, 4 VP) → Руины → Чумной столб → Костница. Put the level-4 buildings on foundations (+4 each).
- Skeletons at 1g plus Курган (+2 back) cleared monsters 2-13 cheaply by round 10. Altar lets me fight while staying put.
- With Капище, a Лич is 5g for 1 VP plus 5 strength. Don't sacrifice Лич on monsters unless the net VP gain is clear.
- The Золен artifact (4 foundations → 2 Лич) is nearly free; grab it once 4 foundations are built.
- Take the cheap fortresses early (Флисс 6, Икара 7) with 8-9 skeletons before opponents do. Гленнфор (10) is a mountain near Нокс.
- Iron is the bottleneck (Алтарь 4, Часовня 5, Столб 5, Склеп 5, Костница 6). As first player, pick iron at a high level. Keep 3 wood spare every build round for a curse (+1 VP each).
- Палач strength = this round's MP; hire them only when build comes before a level-4 move.

## Against Люди
- Humans built many level-4 buildings on foundations and won (50 VP). Башня иллюзий reserves fortresses (they reserved Икара), so take cheap fortresses before they reserve them.
- Their army reaches about 22 by the late game; avoid shared regions with them.

## Against Наги
- The Гидра (18) captures fortresses (Флисс, Бринлор, Юноро) and turns regions into lakes. Take my fortress targets before it does. Nagas rarely threaten my army directly.

## Against Орки
- Orcs take big fortresses (Ролгой, Мослин) and stack foundations, reaching about 50 VP. Their army is often empty after a fortress capture; that is a moment to contest regions safely.
