# Strategy notes: Орки

## General strategy
- Core engine: Идол (R1) → Лагерь → Свалка early. Then EVERY building of level ≥2 goes on a fresh foundation, or one where it raises the level. Свалка refunds 2 rage per placement. Foundations alone were worth 17 VP.
- Path that worked: Лагерь → Качалка (stack on the same foundation) → Мастерская (level 4, 3 VP, blasters 12 vs fortresses) → Дом вождей → Трофейная → Арена/Загон/Металка late on new foundations.
- Army: hire scouts at 2g, upgrade scout→brute for 1 rage (best value). Keep about 10-14 strength ready every move round: monster 2/5/8 and fortresses Ролгой 11, Икара 7, Мослин 21 (blaster 12 + 2 brutes). Don't commit the whole army to a 1 VP monster in R1.
- Rage VP thresholds 12/18/24: plan to HOLD 12+ rage by the end (2 VP). Stop spending rage on raises/upgrades once you are near 12 in the last 3 rounds.
- Содмур artifact (5 victories): claim it as soon as you have 5 victories, because +1 rage per move feeds the threshold. Таскар (12 wood → 6 rage) is good; Дом вождей can raise wood gathering to reach 12.
- Дом вождей: 1 rage = +1 to an action. Use it to complete a building's cost or reach a region, not for plain resources.
- Tiebreak is the stock: in the final round keep leftover resources and rage rather than buying useless units.

## Against Люди
- Люди race buildings and VP (50 VP), get gold from monster wins, and use Башня иллюзий to reserve a fortress you are heading to (they did it to Икара). Take the cheap fortresses first.
- They build a strong army late (22 strength); avoid sharing a region with them in the late game.
- They win ties on stock: keep resources in the final round.

## Against Наги
- Наги mostly score through buildings and Гидра fortress grabs (Флисс, Бринлор, Юноро taken by a lone Гидра). Expect lake-side fortresses to vanish; take your targets first.
- They turn regions into lakes with Гейзер, which can raise your movement costs.

## Against Нежить
- Нежить build a big army fast (skeletons, then Личи at 5 each) and take monsters early. Their curses (Есмор, Икара) cost you your weakest unit on entry: avoid cursed regions with small armies.
- They grab Шог Ид early; don't plan on it.
